Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Tiny Tongues ("the app") is developed by Liza Ma. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Short version

Tiny Tongues does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers of any kind.

What the app does on your device

All flashcard progress and settings are stored locally on your device only. This information never leaves your device and is not visible to us.

Content delivery

The app downloads flashcard images and audio files from a content hosting service so they can be displayed and played. These downloads do not include any personal or device-identifying information — they are the same static files for every user.

Children's privacy

Tiny Tongues is designed to be used by young children together with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing about any user — child or adult — for us to hold, use, or share.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any advertising, analytics, or social SDKs, and does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data — this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to liza.ma@gmail.com.
